Sample HR Policy Document

Leave Policy
Employees are entitled to 12 annual leaves per year, 8 casual leaves, and 10 sick leaves. Leave requests must be submitted at least 3 days in advance. Approval is required from the immediate manager.

Attendance Policy
All employees must check in by 9:30 AM and check out by 6:00 PM. Late arrival more than 3 times a month will result in HR notification.

Expense Reimbursement Policy
Employees must submit expense claims within 10 days of incurring expenses. Original receipts are mandatory. Approval is required from the finance department.

Travel Policy
All official travel must be pre-approved. The company will cover transportation and accommodation costs as per travel grade.

Promotion and Transfer Policy
Promotions are based on performance appraisals conducted annually. Transfer requests must be approved by HR and the relevant department head.

Resignation Policy
Employees must give at least 30 days written notice before resignation. Final settlement will be processed after clearance from all departments.

Work From Home Policy
Employees may work from home up to 4 days per month with prior manager approval.

Code of Conduct
Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior, follow company ethics, and respect colleagues at all times.
